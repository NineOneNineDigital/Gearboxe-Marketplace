# Founding seller outreach and onboarding playbook

**Working playbook and outreach copy for founder review.** No seller recruitment
or outreach is recorded by this document. Outreach requires separate execution
authorization and must respect each community's rules and the recipient's
preferences.

“Founding seller” means optional extra outreach and support. Eligible private
sellers in all 50 U.S. states and Washington, DC can sign up and submit a
listing for the same manual review without an invitation or special access.
Focus proactive outreach on Raleigh–Durham–Chapel Hill personal and local
networks; assist eligible sellers elsewhere in the U.S. on the same terms.
Seek accurate everyday vehicles across broad price bands, and monitor seller
concentration without imposing a minimum number of distinct sellers.

Thirty approved live listings is an inventory-learning milestone, not a
publishing gate or an automatic change to seller access. No date for the
milestone is set. Standard listings and marketplace messaging are free at
launch; do not promise paid listings, future prices, approval, sales, or review
turnaround that has not been staffed and approved.

## Outreach and funnel

1. **Identify:** With execution authorization, identify relevant local personal
   networks and communities and check their consent, solicitation, and posting
   rules. Include strong eligible U.S. sellers outside the Triangle when useful.
   Seek permission before recording a prospect as an assisted recruit.
2. **Invite to learn:** Use approved copy, offer optional help, and make clear
   that the same self-service path is open to eligible sellers nationwide. Record
   opt-in and preferred follow-up channel in an approved private system; respect
   refusal or withdrawal and do not continue unwanted contact.
3. **Qualify and assist:** Explain eligibility and review steps below. Let the
   seller create and control their own account, draft and attestations. Do not
   collect passwords, submit attestations on their behalf, promise approval, or
   bypass ordinary validation or moderation.
4. **Track progress:** Record the stage and next agreed action: opted in,
   drafting, submitted/pending, correction or evidence requested, resubmitted,
   approved/live, or declined/closed. Assign an owner and follow-up date only
   with permission. On rejection, explain the recorded reason and help the seller
   correct and resubmit; moderators retain decision authority. If evidence is
   requested, coordinate privately; a recorded admin request alone may not
   notify the seller.
5. **Follow through:** Once live, show the seller how to manage availability,
   respond to inquiries and report safety concerns. Check in only as agreed and
   record anonymized friction and product follow-ups.

## Assisted onboarding checklist

- Confirm the seller is an adult private seller in an eligible U.S. jurisdiction,
  and the vehicle is an eligible car, truck, SUV, or street-legal specialty
  vehicle. Dealer, motorcycle, RV and parts-only inventory are outside the launch
  scope. Specialty, modified or unusual vehicles may require enhanced review.
- Have the seller verify email and phone, create a draft, and accurately enter
  VIN or eligible pre-1981 serial, vehicle specifications, city/state, mileage
  and odometer status, price, title status, condition, service and modification
  information. The full VIN becomes public on an approved listing.
- Upload at least five distinct, clear, seller-owned photos and label the front,
  rear, left side, right side and interior. Avoid stock or borrowed images,
  misleading duplicates, and private documents. Additional photos are optional.
- Explain that known accidents, damage and mechanical issues must be described.
  A detail may be blank only if the seller knows of no issue to describe; a blank
  is not a marketplace verification of condition.
- Have the seller attest ownership or authorization and accuracy in the safety
  flow. When risk or authorized-seller status calls for evidence, use the private
  document workflow. Branded titles need explicit disclosure and enhanced
  review; outstanding liens require a documented payoff and transfer path.
  Never place ownership or payoff documents in public media or listing text.
- Preview, submit for manual review, and follow any correction request. Drafts
  may remain incomplete; every assisted seller follows the same backend and
  moderation requirements as a self-service seller. Explain that material edits
  return a live listing to review.
- Coach sellers to respond to buyer messages within 24 hours where possible.
  This is an expectation and support practice, not a guaranteed response or
  automatic suspension rule. When a seller misses it, founder or trained backup
  should follow up through an agreed channel, offer help, and record the outcome
  privately. Respect safety, blocking, and notification preferences.
- Show the seller the availability confirmation process: reminder at day 38,
  expiry at day 45 if unconfirmed, pause while reserved, and a fresh period on
  release. Expired listings can be reopened as drafts for review without a fee.

## Private data and measurement

Use an approved access-controlled private system for opt-in, minimal contact
information, listing reference, stage, timestamps, owner, agreed next action,
and follow-up outcome. Do not put contact details, VINs, precise addresses,
messages, ownership or payoff documents, or document contents in Linear. Use
the private evidence workflow and its retention rules; avoid copying documents to
recruitment trackers. Follow the organization's approved retention and deletion
rules and record consent withdrawal. If the approved system or retention owner
is not established, resolve that before collecting prospect data.

Review the funnel using aggregate counts and definitions: opted-in recruits,
drafts started, submissions, corrections/evidence requests, resubmissions,
approved live listings and closed/declined cases. Track opt-in-to-draft and
submission-to-decision times separately, using actual recorded timestamps and
identifying holds; report missing data rather than guessing. Monitor photo and
disclosure quality, price-band variety, seller concentration, response time,
buyer engagement, and support workload. Record recurring friction without
identifying details and link focused product issues; do not infer seller quality
or dealer status from listing count alone.

At 30 approved live listings, review those measures and moderation/support
capacity with the founder. Record what was observed, data gaps, decisions,
owners, and follow-up dates. This review does not change self-service access or
replace the separate public launch readiness decision.
