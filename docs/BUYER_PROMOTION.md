# Nationwide buyer promotion plan

**Draft for founder approval.** No campaign has been launched, assets approved,
channels booked, or external messages sent under this plan. The founder must
approve the channel owners, audience mix, creative and soft-launch go/no-go
before publication. Paid spend is **$0 for the first 30 days**: no ads, boosts,
or paid placements. After 30 days, consider a separate founder spend decision
only with qualified-conversation conversion data; no spend is authorized here.

## Objective and audience

Help U.S. buyers discover available private-party vehicles and start useful
buyer–seller exchanges. Reach buyers nationally through organic, social and
community channels while being clear that initial inventory may be sparse and
concentrated around Raleigh–Durham–Chapel Hill. Do not imply nationwide supply
density or that a vehicle is nearby without checking current inventory. Thirty
approved listings is an inventory-learning milestone, not a campaign threshold
or seller access gate.

Eligible U.S. private sellers can self-service list without an invitation;
Triangle founding-seller outreach offers optional assistance on the same review
terms. Browsing, standard listings and marketplace messaging are free at launch.
Gearboxe does not handle vehicle-purchase payment or guarantee condition,
ownership, inspection, title transfer, or a completed sale. Popup events and
partner services are not part of this marketplace launch.

## Entry experience and launch checks

Before any promotion, the founder records the [soft-launch go/no-go](https://linear.app/mxndev/issue/GBXMRKT-19/run-public-soft-launch-readiness-review-and-founder-gono-go).
Verify the reviewed production release, approved policy and consent, seller and
buyer paths, safety/support readiness, staffed moderation and backup, and the
end-to-end browser rehearsal. A working public inventory page must show
nationwide listings before ZIP entry, offer ZIP-first search with a visible
50-mile default after entry, and make broadening to all U.S. inventory obvious
when nearby results are empty. Verify these behaviors on mobile and desktop,
including sparse areas, invalid ZIPs and no results; do not claim them merely
because this plan describes them.

Check canonical links and search indexing, social preview title/image, mobile
layout, sign-in and verified-email messaging, seller signup, moderation status,
email delivery and support copy. Confirm the promoted URL is live and the
inventory examples and locations are current. Record any failure and block
promotion until resolved or the founder explicitly changes the go/no-go.

## Controlled schedule and pause

- **Before launch:** Founder approves owners, copy, assets, community rules,
  attribution design, coverage and rollback contact. No public promotion before
  the go decision.
- **First week:** Publish a small, approved organic set in stages. The named
  campaign owner (TBD) reviews inventory accuracy, qualified exchanges, spam and
  fraud reports, support demand, email delivery and moderation backlog daily;
  log observations, sample sizes and action owners. The founder or named backup
  must be reachable to pause placements and respond to incidents.
- **Days 8–30:** Review weekly by geographic audience and message with context
  on inventory and samples. Adjust organic activity only within approved copy
  and community rules. Paid spend stays $0.
- **Pause:** The campaign owner or founder stops scheduled posts and asks
  community owners to remove or correct placements when links fail, inventory
  claims become misleading, fraud/spam rises, or support/moderation exceeds
  capacity. Escalate to the founder and safety lead, record incident and owner,
  and use the deployment rollback procedure if the product itself is affected.
  Resume only after corrective checks and founder approval.

## Measurement and privacy

The lead outcome is a **qualified buyer–seller exchange**: a verified buyer's
first inquiry about an Active listing followed by a seller response, including
an offer response. Count Reserved availability questions separately. The
existing directional target of ten qualified exchanges in 30 days is for
learning, not a release gate or a guaranteed outcome.

The admin engagement report omits pairs an operator explicitly excludes as
test traffic or confirmed abuse. Exclusion and restoration require an audited
reason; the report shows the excluded-pair count alongside included aggregates.
The bounded review list contains only opaque pair, listing, and buyer IDs plus
inquiry type, source, time, response status, and exclusion state. Operators
should use case references rather than message text, offer details, or contact
information in audit reasons. Unreviewed test or spam activity may still count.

Use approved, privacy-reviewed internal attribution and aggregate counts for
landing visits → ZIP searches → approved-listing views → verified buyer signups
→ first inquiries → seller replies → qualified exchanges. Define event sources,
deduplication, time windows and missing instrumentation before comparing rates;
this document does not assert those events are implemented. Break down results
by broad geographic audience and message only where samples and privacy rules
permit; show counts, denominators, dates, and sparse-inventory context. Clicks
and signups are supporting signals, not proof of channel success. Track support
load, moderation age, fraud and unanswered inquiries alongside conversion.

Do not send full ZIP codes, message text, private documents, or contact details
to ad or analytics vendors without separate privacy approval. Do not infer a
person's exact location from campaign attribution. Confirm privacy, retention,
consent and access controls before enabling any new tracking.
